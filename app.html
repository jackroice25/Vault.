Plans & features
Every Vault feature and the plan it comes with.
Keeper · Collector · Vaulter — pick what fits.
Active item limits
Free20 items Keeper75 items Collector250 items VaulterUnlimited
Archived items don't count. If you downgrade or cancel, nothing is ever deleted: all your items stay visible and editable — you just can't add new ones until you're back under your plan's limit.
1
Shared & collaborative listsKEEPER
Shared lists in the sidebar / bottom menu
Invite anyone into a list and edit it together in real time. Perfect for couples furnishing a home, a group planning a trip, or a family registry, everyone adds items and everyone sees the updates live.
Creating shared lists needs Keeper or above; invited guests can view and edit the list you share with them for free.
2
Gift modeKEEPER
Gift lists
Publish a wishlist as a gift list and share one link, no account needed to open it. Guests quietly reserve what they'll buy, so two people never gift the same thing. Ideal for birthdays and the holidays.
You stay surprised: reservations are hidden from you and visible only to your guests.
3
Savings goalsKEEPER
On each product
Set an amount to put aside toward something you want and watch a progress bar fill as you save. A calm, motivating way to buy on purpose instead of on impulse.
Track savings per item and mark each contribution as you go.
4
RemindersKEEPER
On each product
Add a reminder to any item, tomorrow, next week, end of the month, or a date you choose. When it's time, Vault brings it back to the top so nothing you meant to buy quietly slips away.
You set the reminder date yourself; Vault surfaces the item when it's due.
5
One-tap saveFREE
iOS share sheet & the paste bar
On iPhone, share any page to Vault straight from Safari's share menu, no copy-paste. On the web, paste a link into the bar at the top and it's saved instantly, with title, image and price filled in.
The Vault share extension adds “Vault” to the iOS share menu.
6
Price historyCOLLECTOR
On each product
Note the prices you spot over time and Vault keeps a history for each item, plus a “price found” field to record a deal and see your saving as a percentage, so you buy at the right moment.
Prices are the ones you record yourself, Vault does not scrape live prices automatically.
7
Taste InsightsCOLLECTOR
Profile → Taste Insights
Analyses all saved products and builds a personal style profile. It calculates your dominant price range (budget, mid-range, premium), favourite category, ratio of explored vs purchased products, starred favourites, average wishlist price, and conversion rate from “want it” to “bought”.
Computed on your own device from your own vault. Needs at least 3 products for meaningful results.
8
Advanced statisticsCOLLECTOR
Statistics in the sidebar
Detailed metrics: average spend by category, product distribution by price range, purchase rate (how many wishlist items get bought), products with the biggest savings vs original price, and a monthly purchase summary.
On Free and Keeper this section appears locked. With Collector or Vaulter the lock disappears and your real data becomes visible.
9
Advanced mood boardsVAULTER
Mood Board view (layers icon)
The basic mood board shows products as side-by-side images. The Vaulter version adds fullscreen mode with free drag-and-drop layout, text labels and captions over images, manual card resizing, and export of the board as a PNG image.
The basic mood board view stays available to everyone. Advanced editing activates automatically with Vaulter.
10
Premium themesVAULTER
Settings → Theme
Exclusive colour palettes for the whole interface: monochromatic, warm and cool variants, light backgrounds with coloured accents and more. Each theme consistently restyles background, borders, text and accents everywhere.
Available now, pick your palette in Settings → Theme.
11
Cloud syncFREE
Automatic with your account
Sign in and your vault follows you, everything stays in sync across your phone, tablet and computer. Prefer to stay anonymous? Use Vault as a guest and your data lives locally on this device.
Sync is active whenever you're signed in with your account.
12
Early access to new featuresVAULTER
Automatic with Vaulter
Every new feature reaches Vaulter members first, new shop integrations, new item types, new views and interface improvements. You don't need to do anything: features appear in the app as soon as they're released.
Features marked as early access in the menu are previews reserved for Vaulter.
