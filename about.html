About Vault

Vault is an independent product, built in Italy, for one simple idea: everything you want — products, places, gifts, trips — deserves one beautiful, private home instead of a hundred screenshots and forgotten tabs.

Why we built it

Wishing for things is scattered across screenshots, notes, open tabs and "save for later" folders you never open again. Vault turns that mess into a single, considered place: paste a link and it fills in the title, image and price for you; organise by section; switch between grid, list, globe and moodboard views; and keep it in sync across your devices.

Places matter as much as products. A restaurant you want to try, a hotel for next summer, a city on your list — Vault saves them too, and drops them on a globe you can actually browse.

What we believe

  • Private by default. Nothing you save is public unless you decide to turn on a public profile or share a list. There are no ads, and no advertising or third-party behavioural tracking.
  • Your data is yours. Export everything to CSV, or delete your account entirely, at any time.
  • Beautiful is not optional. A wishlist is personal. It should feel considered, calm and yours — not like a spreadsheet.
  • Honest about what's ready. Every feature is marked Available, Beta or Coming soon — the same way in the app and on this site.

How Vault makes money

Vault is free forever for the core experience. Vault Plus (€4.99/month or €49.99/year) is optional and funds development — it adds sharing, gift lists, reminders, price history, advanced moodboards, premium themes and taste insights. We don't sell your data; that's not our business model, and it never will be.
