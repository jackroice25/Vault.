Help Center

Everything you need to get the most out of Vault. Can't find an answer? Email vaultwishlist@gmail.com.

Getting started

1. Save your first thing

Copy any product or place link and paste it into the bar at the top. Vault fills in the title, image and price for you.

2. Organise it

Create sections (Fashion, Tech, Restaurants…), star favourites, and switch between grid, list, globe and moodboard views.

3. Keep it in sync

Use it as a guest, or create a free account to sync privately across all your devices.

4. Share when you want

Turn on a public profile for free; with a paid plan, share a list or start a gift registry friends can reserve from.

Saving from the web

Pasting a link

Paste any URL into the top bar and Vault reads the page for you. It works on most shops, marketplaces, Google Maps, Booking and more.

On iPhone

Open the page in Safari, tap the Share button, then choose Vault — the item lands in your vault without copy-paste.

When a site blocks automatic reading

A few large brands block bots. Vault still saves the item with a clean name from the link; just add the photo and price yourself in a couple of seconds.

Frequently asked questions

Is Vault free?
Yes — the core experience is free forever (up to 20 active items). Paid plans are optional and start at €2.99/month: Keeper (sharing, gift lists, savings goals, reminders), Collector (250 items, + price history, taste insights, advanced statistics) and Vaulter (unlimited items, the globe map, advanced moodboards, premium themes).
Is my data private?
Private by default — you control what becomes public. Nothing is shown to anyone unless you turn on a public profile or share a list. No ads, no advertising or third-party behavioural tracking. See our Privacy Policy.
How do public profiles work?
Optional. You can turn on a public profile (nickname, avatar, bio) and mark selected items as public or "featured". Everything else stays private. You can make your profile private again or clear it anytime.
How do I share a list or make a gift registry?
Starting with Keeper: shared lists let you invite people and edit together in real time; gift lists let you share one link so friends quietly reserve what they'll buy — nobody duplicates a gift.
What is "price history"?
Vault records the price it reads when you save an item, and any prices you enter later, so you can see how it changed over time. Vault does not continuously scrape live prices in the background.
How do I export or delete my data?
In Settings: Export CSV downloads your items; Delete account removes everything, including cloud data.
How do I cancel my subscription?
On the web, open Manage subscription in your account — you keep your plan until the end of the paid period. If you subscribed via the App Store, cancel in your Apple ID subscription settings.
Which devices does Vault run on?
Any modern browser on web, a free desktop app for Mac and Windows, and iPhone (native app in TestFlight, App Store on the way). Everything syncs through your account.
