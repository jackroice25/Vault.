How it works

  1. Save the product by pasting its link — Vault fills in photo, title and price.
  2. Leave it marked as "want it". Vault checks the shop's price once a day.
  3. When the price falls by 5% or more, you get a notification with the old and new price.
  4. Tap it to open the item, see the full price history and go buy it.

Why a wishlist beats a price tracker

Price trackers only watch prices. Vault Wishlist is where you already keep everything you want — products, places, gift ideas — organised in sections, shareable as a gift list, private by default. Price-drop alerts are one more reason to save it there instead of in twenty open tabs.

Price-drop alerts are included in the Collector (€4.99/month) and Vaulter plans. They work on most shops that show a price on the product page; a few large brands block automatic reading.
