Questions

Do I lose anything if I cancel or downgrade?
No. Nothing is ever deleted: all your items stay visible and editable. If you're over your plan's limit you just can't add new items until you're back under it.

How do price-drop alerts work?
With Collector or Vaulter, Vault checks the price of the things you want once a day and notifies you when it drops by 5% or more. More about price-drop alerts.

Can I try it first?
Yes — the Free plan has no time limit and no card. You can also use Vault as a guest without an account.

Is my wishlist private?
Private by default. Nothing is public unless you turn on a public profile or share a list. No behavioural advertising, no third-party tracking.
