Make your Christmas list in 3 minutes

  1. Create a free Vault and paste the links of the gifts you'd love.
  2. Open Gift lists and pick the items for your Christmas list.
  3. Share the link in the family chat or by email.
  4. Everyone reserves privately — you'll only find out on Christmas morning.

Tips for a better Christmas wishlist

Mix price ranges so everyone can find something. Add the size, colour or variant in the note so nobody has to guess. Include one or two experiences — a restaurant or a trip — not just objects: Vault saves places too.
