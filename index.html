01 The idea

Stop screenshotting.Start saving.

We save things everywhere, a screenshot here, a browser tab there, a note we never open again. Vault gathers all of it into one calm place you'll actually return to: beautiful to look at, effortless to keep, quietly organised.

02 How saving works

From any shopto your Vault.

No forms, no typing. A link is all Vault needs to turn something you liked into something you'll find again.

i.

Spot it anywhere.

A jacket in a boutique, a lamp on a marketplace, a restaurant on a map. If it lives on a web page, it can live in Vault.

ii.

Copy the link.

One tap on Share, or a copy from the address bar. No screenshots, no tabs left open for weeks.

iii.

Paste it into Vault.

Vault reads the page and fills in the photo, the title and the price for you. On sites that block automatic reading it still saves a clean title you can finish in seconds.

iv.

It's in your wishlist.

A considered card, not a row in a sheet: photo, price, shop and status, waiting for the next time you look.

v.

Organise it. Find it again.

Drop it into a section, mark it want · ordered · bought, and search your whole Vault in a keystroke.

08 Questions

Good to know.

Is Vault really free?
Yes. The free plan is free forever (up to 50 active items), save products and places from most sites, organise them into sections, use the grid, list and moodboard views, and sync privately across your devices. Paid plans are optional and start at €2.99/month (Keeper), with Collector (€4.99) and Vaulter (€9.99) adding more items and features.
How does saving from a website work?
Copy the link of anything you like, a product, a restaurant, a hotel, and paste it into Vault. It reads the page and fills in the title, image and price automatically. With the Vault iPhone app (in beta now, on the App Store in early 2027) you can also save straight from Safari's share menu.
Which websites are supported?
Most of the web works out of the box, shops, marketplaces, Google Maps, Booking and countless others. A few large brands use aggressive bot protection that can block automatic reading; on those, Vault still saves the item with a clean name from the link, and you can add the photo and price yourself in a couple of seconds.
What do the paid plans include?
Keeper (€2.99/mo) adds 150 items, shared lists, unlimited gift lists, savings goals and reminders. Collector (€4.99/mo) adds 300 items, automatic price-drop alerts, taste insights and advanced statistics. Vaulter (€9.99/mo) unlocks unlimited items, the advanced globe (status rings and custom colours), advanced moodboards, premium themes and early access to new features. Cloud sync and one-tap save are free for everyone. See the pricing table for the full breakdown.
Can I share a list with other people?
Yes. Your first gift list is free; shared lists start with the Keeper plan. Shared lists let you invite anyone and edit together in real time, ideal for couples, families or a group trip. Gift lists go a step further: share one link (no account needed to open it) and friends quietly reserve what they'll buy, so nobody gifts the same thing twice.
Is my data private?
Private by default, you control what becomes public. No behavioural advertising, and no third-party tracking. Your data is yours, passwords are securely hashed, everything travels encrypted, and you can export all of it to CSV or delete your account at any time. Read our Privacy Policy for the details.
Do I need to install anything?
No. Vault runs in your browser, nothing to download. You can use it as a guest with everything stored locally, or create a free account to sync across devices. There's also a free desktop app for Mac and Windows. The iPhone app is in private beta and arrives on the App Store in early 2027; until then Vault works in Safari on iPhone (Share → Add to Home Screen).
